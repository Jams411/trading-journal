The accounting row above (Gross P&L → Trading Costs → Trading Net P&L) is a separate, fully broker-reconciled ₹ view across both brokers' lifetime records — it does not share the capital-proxy denominator used for Net Return above and is not a return percentage. ₹9,98,843.30 − ₹3,02,806.54 = ₹6,96,036.76, itemized in Broker Reconciliation below.

These figures are P&L after Trading Costs for both brokers, corrected from a previously published mixed-basis calculation that combined Angel One's net P&L with Zerodha's gross (pre-cost) P&L for 17 of these 25 months — that earlier version reported +39.48% and an apparent +16.46pp of outperformance, which does not survive on a cost-consistent basis. On a continuous Jul 2023–Aug 2025 Nifty 50 benchmark that does not skip April 2025, net relative performance is −5.99pp. The capital-proxy denominator (an event-weighted mean ledger balance, not time-weighted deployed capital) remains unvalidated for return calculation — these are historical, reproducible figures, not canonical continuous account returns.

A before-Trading-Costs (gross) version of this same 25-observation series was investigated and could not be reconstructed: Angel One's dated, realization-timestamped records cover only a ~2.5-month execution window, while its controlling P&L population (the source of gross figures) carries no realization dates at all across the other ~11.5 months this series needs. Publishing an estimated gross figure to fill that gap would not meet this project's evidence standard, so only the after-Trading-Costs series is shown here. Canonical lifetime Gross P&L is reconciled separately in the accounting row above and in Broker Reconciliation below.

Evidence and accounting warning · methodology correction. The 25 selected observations below are P&L after Trading Costs for both brokers, using the same capital-proxy denominator and compounding method throughout. This series was corrected from an earlier version that combined Angel One's net P&L with Zerodha's gross (pre-cost) P&L for 17 of the 25 months, which overstated the compounded return.

The earlier mixed-basis version reported +39.48% portfolio return and +16.46 percentage points of apparent outperformance versus Nifty 50 — that version is retired and does not appear as a current figure anywhere on this site. On the corrected after-costs basis, the portfolio returns +21.28% and trails the same 25-month Nifty 50 chain (+23.02%) by −1.73 percentage points (−5.99pp against a continuous Jul 2023–Aug 2025 Nifty benchmark that does not skip April 2025).

The capital-proxy denominator itself (an event-weighted mean of ledger balances, not time-weighted deployed capital) remains unvalidated for return calculation — April 2025 is still omitted and Angel One's full controlling population still cannot be dated. A genuine before-Trading-Costs (gross) reconstruction of this series was investigated and could not be supported on the same observation dates for the same reason (see Historical Performance section above). These figures remain historical public-series outputs, not authoritative combined net-performance metrics.
