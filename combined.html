Evidence and accounting warning · methodology correction

The 25 selected observations below are P&L after Trading Costs for both brokers, using the same capital-proxy denominator and compounding method as before. This series was corrected from an earlier version that combined Angel One's net P&L with Zerodha's gross (pre-cost) P&L for 17 of the 25 months, which overstated the compounded return. The earlier mixed-basis version reported +39.48% portfolio return and +16.46 percentage points of apparent outperformance versus Nifty 50. On the corrected after-costs basis, the portfolio returns +21.28% and trails the same 25-month Nifty 50 chain (+23.02%) by −1.73 percentage points (−5.99pp against a continuous Jul 2023–Aug 2025 Nifty benchmark that does not skip April 2025). The capital-proxy denominator itself (an event-weighted mean of ledger balances, not time-weighted deployed capital) remains unvalidated for return calculation — April 2025 is still omitted and Angel One's full controlling population still cannot be dated. These figures remain historical public-series outputs, not authoritative combined net-performance metrics.
